The thin bar pinned at the top is a reading-progress bar: it fills as this box is scrolled, and it is the one form of progress that is not a progress element, since there is no value to announce.

The bar under it is the ordinary kind, a native progress element with a value, drawn in the hue.

Scroll on and the top bar fills; scroll back and it empties. It follows the reader's own hand and never moves by itself, which is why it stays on under reduced motion.

Where a scroll timeline is unsupported the top bar is not shown at all. A bar that never moved would say the reader had not started.
